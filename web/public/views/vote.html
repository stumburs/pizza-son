    How scoring works

    Each round you rank 5 berts. The top bert gets 4 points, then 3, 2, 1, and the bottom bert gets 0. Points are added up across all rounds and all voters. Bert seen in more rounds can score more, which is expected: regular appearances near the top are what makes a bert the best overall.
